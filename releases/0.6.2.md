## YouTube Music alongside Plex

- Connect a YouTube Music account from the top bar, with a persistent on-device session and background refresh.
- Mix optional YTM recommendations, releases, mixes and listening history with Plex's home hubs. Open dedicated hub pages to browse beyond the home preview.
- Browse YTM artists, albums and playlists alongside Plex, including library playlists and liked songs. Search both catalogs, prefer Plex matches, and fill missing album tracks from YTM.
- Play mixed queues through Hibiki's native audio engine. Reuse cached audio, loudness and waveform analysis for fast repeat playback and quick resume, with shared analysis fallbacks for Plex tracks.
- Show service logos, cached cover art, YTM hearts and source details throughout the queue and track information views.
- Prefer Plex destinations for artist and album links, with YTM destinations for music outside the Plex library.
- Align YTM right-click menus with Plex's shared layout, playback actions, radio, sharing and navigation.

## Radio

- Use the headphone menu for Plex radio, YTM radio or a combined station, independently of Guest DJ.
- Keep ten songs ahead. Combined radio alternates five Plex and five YTM recommendations; a seed missing from the other catalog uses ten recommendations from its own service.
- Refill as the queue progresses, with duplicate suppression, cached preparation and a retry action when recommendations cannot load.

## Client reuse and performance

- Share playback preparation, metadata hydration and collection pagination across library and streaming sources.
- Bound catalog caches and use stale-while-revalidate updates for YTM browsing.
- Preserve saved queue artwork and source metadata, and defer expensive artwork resolution until cards approach the viewport.

YouTube Music integration is unofficial and unsupported by Google. Playback tools are installed separately from the connection panel.
