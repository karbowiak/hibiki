## A new look for Hibiki

Artwork now carries through the window, with sharp artist/album heroes, a blurred
continuation, translucent navigation and colors drawn from the active image.
Playback controls keep their own now-playing palette. Browsing pages can rotate
backgrounds, and cards, loading states, search and contextual actions share the
same visual language.

## Metadata and playlists

- Liked Songs now saves hearts locally in Hibiki, with Loved/Rated views and
  optional two-way connections to Last.fm, ListenBrainz and a regular Plex
  Liked Songs playlist. Connections start disabled; unmatched favorites stay
  saved for review, with account checks and recovery for interrupted changes.
- Redesigned artist/album/playlist editors with reviewed Plex saves, editable
  tags and locks, separate posters/backgrounds and artwork uploads.
- Canonical MusicBrainz/Heya identity review and optional explicit Heya discovery.
- SoulSync album/track enrichment and optional premium TheAudioDB metadata/artwork.
- Playlist-level sync controls with manual Plex → ListenBrainz as the default,
  optional two-way sync, and one ListenBrainz browser for owned, generated and
  collaborative playlists.
- Dynamic Plex playlists can export their current songs to ListenBrainz;
  sync requires fresh online reads and keeps smart playlists read-only.
- Playlist setup can create a new private ListenBrainz destination before
  recording review, or link an existing playlist.
- Create Sync immediately creates a local playlist and matches songs in the
  background. Matching handles credits, punctuation and equivalent library
  copies; unresolved songs remain available for later review.
- Matching continues past unavailable lookups and through repeated-song batches.
  Reliable matches are saved, remaining songs stay in Sync review, and completed
  partial results remain in each playlist's Sync review with a yellow indicator.
- Bounded artist-catalogue candidate lookup improves crowded-title discovery
  while preserving recording, version and duration checks.
- Source configuration dialogs sit beside metadata priority controls, including
  SoulSync, with status indicators and keyboard reordering.
- Stable Weekly Exploration and Weekly Jams channels follow new editions into
  the same local playlists while Hibiki is open or on next launch. Edition dates
  appear in descriptions.
- Ordered verification, conflict review and durable recovery prevent duplicate
  creation and blind retries. Unsupported repeated Plex occurrences remain in
  review while other reliable matches can be imported.

## Reliability and packaging

- Full collection queues with virtualized rows and bounded song-detail loading.
  Shuffle includes the complete known collection immediately. Music queues save
  their order, current song and playback position per Plex library and restore
  paused after reopening Hibiki.
- Pinned queues reopen docked with their saved tab. Routine scrobbling retries,
  archived account/library reports and matching gaps stay quiet; sync review
  appears on the relevant playlist or Liked Songs Sync button. A scrobbling
  outage raises one dismissible notice after 24 hours of failed retries.

- Plex-first connected browsing with bounded visited-page caches and focused,
  account/server/library-scoped SQLite snapshots of playlists and favorites.
  Collection snapshots refresh at launch, periodically and after edits.
- Virtual track lists and album/artist grids retain the full collection count
  and scrollbar while loading only nearby pages, including arbitrary jumps.
  Cached membership supports whole-collection shuffle with upcoming metadata
  fetched in bounded chunks. Live favorites updates retain visible rows.
- Offline browsing and playback of complete cached/downloaded music when Plex
  is unavailable, including seeking, saved waveforms and available analysis.
  Unavailable tracks are grayed out and playlist changes stay read-only offline.
- Download controls and status icons for songs, albums, artists and playlists.
  Explicit downloads are kept separately from the evictable playback cache;
  shared collection downloads retain their ownership when another is removed.
- Downloaded collections check for new songs at startup and hourly while
  connected. Playlist Info sits beside More, with named download errors, retry
  and sync review; quick hover panels share the app's glossy surfaces.
- Downloads live in the system Music folder under Hibiki, organized by artist
  and album. Settings can move them to another location. Existing downloads
  migrate with verified copies, retaining saved analysis; downloaded playlists
  get portable relative-path M3U files that update as songs become available.
- Legacy download claims are adopted by verified collections, so removing an
  old playlist download releases its songs while preserving shared downloads.
- Downloaded music retains its private Plex metadata, saved waveforms and
  available analysis. Collection caching resumes interrupted pages without a
  full-library metadata or waveform worker.
- Independent database reads keep browsing and playback responsive during
  cache updates. Artist/album content appears before optional recommendations, and
  large playlist pages avoid walking earlier entries.
- Loudness is prepared before playback and during queue preloading. Tracks with
  existing gain retain streaming startup; missing gain is analyzed before the
  track begins rather than changing its volume after playback starts.
- Large genre inventories remain searchable with bounded card pagination, and
  playlist cards use verified membership totals when Plex's count is stale.
- Non-destructive album/media-version comparison and current Plex track refetch
  before playback of sanitized cached metadata.
- Correct Plex library identity and current poster-inventory parsing, including
  local artwork previews. Playlist creation uses the correct ordered track URI
  and makes a single request.
- Search text is correctly URL-encoded, and verified playlist updates refresh
  the affected page without leaving an empty cached result behind.
- Playlist backgrounds use artwork from their own members, with neutral
  loading/error states and scoped image transitions.
- AppStream metadata, refreshed interface previews and verified AppImageUpdate
  `.zsync` companions alongside signed Tauri updater artifacts.
- Release checks validate macOS disk images and all nine installer signatures
  before publication, with signed version checks in the application updater.

Existing appearance and playback settings are retained. Optional metadata
services require configuration, and linked sync requires explicit setup. AI
features are deferred; this release does not install or download a local model.
